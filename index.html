Common questions

Before you press send.

Quick answers to the questions new clients ask most. Anything else, the request desk replies fast.

What can Mexus source?add

Anything within our six supply lines above — and quite a lot outside them. If an item isn’t listed, send it anyway; sourcing unusual requirements is part of the service.

Can I combine several categories in one request?add

Yes — that’s the point. Related requirements are consolidated into a single quotation so you deal with one supplier instead of five.

What should my request include?add

Item names or specifications, quantities, preferred brands where it matters, the delivery location, and your expected timing. Rough lists are fine — we’ll tidy them up with you.

Does support continue after delivery?add

Yes. Equipment repair, maintenance, spares, and repeat procurement all stay with the same desk that handled your original order.
