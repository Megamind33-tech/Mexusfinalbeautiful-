Common questions

Before you send a supply request.

Provide the clearest product description, quantities, preferred brands, location, and timing available.

What categories can Mexus source?add

Office equipment and furniture, printing systems, computer accessories, hardware, vehicle spares, uniforms, PPE, cleaning materials, hospital materials, agriculture inputs, school furniture, maintenance, foodstuffs, beverages, and trade brokerage.

Can I request several categories together?add

Yes. Related requirements can be consolidated into one request to reduce fragmented sourcing and repeated follow-up.

What information should I include?add

Include item names or specifications, quantities, preferred brands where relevant, required delivery location, and expected timing.

Can Mexus support ongoing maintenance?add

Yes. Requests can include equipment repair, maintenance, spares, and follow-on operational support where relevant.
